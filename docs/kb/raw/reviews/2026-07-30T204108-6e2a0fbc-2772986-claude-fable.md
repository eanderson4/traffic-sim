1. `engine/gridlock.go:185` + `engine/rightofway.go:271-277` — HeldTurn is zeroed one hop too early in the in-box call. `exitBlocked`'s probe zeroes `HeldTurn` on the premise that "reaching `next` already consumed the held turn" — true for the entry-time call, where the walk starts at a lane v hasn't entered. In the in-box call the walk starts at v's *current* lane, so the first hop out of it is exactly the crossing that would consume the held turn (`engine.go:760`). Consequence: `jammedAtJunction` picks the arm using `pickSuccessor(lane, v)` with v's real HeldTurn (`gridlock.go:173`), but `exitBlocked` walks the route/default branch — on a multi-successor internal lane with a turn intent issued in-box, the arm decision and the walked chain can disagree (sealed routed branch never rescued, or draining routed branch spuriously strands). This is the wrong-branch class ADR-0032 removed, and `leaderAtFor` documents the correct discipline: spend the turn on the first hop, zero after (`engine.go:456-465`). If netimport never emits multi-successor internal lanes, "too early to need this" is a defensible triage — but record it, because hand-authored NetFiles aren't so constrained.
