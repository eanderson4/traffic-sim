## Findings

**No blockers.** The concurrency design holds up under scrutiny:

- The `closed`-latch double-check in `startGuarded` (supervisor.go:222,237) is sound under Go's seq-cst atomics: a start that passes the second `closed` check has its generation covered by `shutdownFinal`'s `abortGen` stamp (supervisor.go:339), and a start that completes readiness before the stamp lands is reaped by `shutdownFinal`'s `stopLocked` once it wins `mu`. No interleaving orphans a child.
- The reaper now clearing `s.active` (supervisor.go:273) is safe for all other readers: `stopLocked` no-ops on nil (correct — child already reaped), `withActiveReplay` already checked `a.done`, and the `handleStart` supersede check reads the mirror.
- Gate coverage is complete: all seven mutating POSTs are wrapped (main.go:344-356), 401 precedes the handler's 404 so unauthenticated probes can't enumerate demo/recording ids, and the internal autostart path bypasses the gate by construction.
