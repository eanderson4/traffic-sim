- **blocker** `engine/metrics.go:602` — `Finalize` never verifies that the horizon tick was observed. After `Step(2)` without `Observe(2)`, it stamps trips at tick 2 and closes intervals through tick 2 using accumulations only through tick 1, silently diluting metrics. Require `e.Tick == lastTick` when `e.Tick > 0`.
- **blocker** `engine/metrics.go:979` — Spawner backlog recomputes the entire schedule lag using the origin’s current rate. A `DemandSchedule` rate change therefore retroactively increases/decreases already accrued latent demand; pending backlog can jump or shrink without service. Track/integrate backlog across rate changes rather than `lag × current rate`.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:309` — Interval wire bounds are shifted one tick: a window beginning at tick 0 emits `[1, PeriodTicks+1)`, contrary to ADR-0014’s tick-0-aligned bounds. Consumers converting bounds to simulation time get both endpoints offset by `dt`.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:331` — Initial stopped vehicles seed `prev.stopped=true`, so remaining stopped on tick 1 does not count an episode start. Their trip can report positive `StoppedTimeS` but zero stops, and lane interval stop counts omit the start-of-run episode.
- **question** `engine/metrics.go:465` — Spawn-and-despawn within one tick is knowingly invisible, violating ADR-0014’s “every vehicle” trip/totals primitive. The stated ≤4.3 m assumption depends on `dt=0.1`, while ADR-0005 permits larger ticks and imported networks can contain short lanes; is the engine going to expose lifecycle events to the observer before this kernel ships?
