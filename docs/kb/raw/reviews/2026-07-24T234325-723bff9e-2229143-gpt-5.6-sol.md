- **blocker** `engine/natsio/driver/driver.go:325` — The per-replica budget makes route-application timing depend on fleet partitioning and failover. A deferred vehicle can pass a fork under default routing; if its controller fails before admission, process-local `wantLane` is lost and the replacement captures a later lane (`engine/natsio/driver/driver.go:412`). Freezing the lane within one replica does not preserve ADR-0008’s failover behavior. This consequential divergence needs a design fix or ADR update.
- **blocker** `engine/natsio/driver/driver.go:193` — Retrying hello on every request error replays a non-idempotent operation. A request may time out after the engine creates a controller (`engine/natsio/contract.go:489`); retrying creates a ghost drive controller whose capacity corrupts pause/resume accounting and which persists indefinitely in unpaced mode. Retry only definite pre-delivery failures such as `ErrNoResponders`, or make hello idempotent via an ADR-gated contract change.
