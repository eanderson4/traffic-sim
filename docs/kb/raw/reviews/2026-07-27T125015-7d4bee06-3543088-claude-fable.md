## Blocker

**1. `bottleneck_town.py` lane indexing is mirrored — the generated junctions violate the scenario's own "phases are conflict-free by construction" invariant.**
`scripts/demos/bottleneck_town.py:285` offsets lane `i` by `-(i+0.5)·LANE_W` along the **left** normal, so index 0 is the *innermost (left)* lane and the highest index is the curb — the opposite of the docstring at `:279`/`:477` ("index 0 = rightmost") and of the SUMO convention the format requires (`engine/netfile.go:52`; the sibling `merge-pod.py` gets this right by sorting slots by y). Everything keyed on that convention is therefore mirrored: right turns depart the inside lane (`right_lane=0`, `:463`), and the "left-turn bay" (`build_piece` flare, "extra lanes on the LEFT") is built on the curb side. Confirmed in the generated base network: eastbound approach at J1 has idx 0 at y=−301.75 (innermost), idx 2 = the bay at y=−308.75 (curbside); geometric intersection tests show `iJ1_W0_S0` (right turn) **crosses** `iJ1_W1_E1` (through) *inside the shared `main_thru` green*, and the opposing protected lefts `iJ1_W2_N0` × `iJ1_E2_S0` **cross each other** inside the shared `main_left` green. `foesCross`/`foesMerge` are left empty at signalized junctions explicitly on the strength of the conflict-free claim (`bottleneck_town.py:27-40`, `docs/show/bottleneck-town.md:66`), so these crossings are unarbitrated.

Mitigating facts, stated so you can triage: idle runs measure 0–1 collision observations and every arm shares the defect, so the shipped A/B deltas very likely survive; nothing in the engine reads edgeIndex handedness (adjacency only). But (a) the module docstring, `docs/show/bottleneck-town.md`, and `town_report.py:141` ("0 = kerbside") now ship claims the network demonstrably falsifies; (b) it's a latent collision source at higher demand or with box spillback; (c) it's *visible* in the baked replay and diagrams this commit durably binds — every right-turner swings from the fast lane across the slow lane, in a demo whose pitch is realism. Fix is one sign in `add_edge` (`off = -(nlanes-i-0.5)*LANE_W`) plus a pod re-run and artifact regeneration.

## Should-fix

**2. `ObsBridgeTicks` overstates the healable outage for any cadence > 1** (`engine/natsio/contract.go:401-417`). Obs frames go out every tick per drive controller (`contract.go:885-898`), so a streak of r failed publishes = r blind ticks — good, units match. But hold-last covers `(k−1)+H` ticks *from the last fresh intent*: if the outage covers a cadence-due tick (worst case), the intent gap is `k+r`, so the fleet coasts as soon as `r > HoldLastTicks`, independent of k. The bridge `(fastest−1)+HoldLastTicks` is the best-case alignment; only at cadence 1 does it equal the safe bound. `contract_test.go`'s final assertion (bridge = 6 at cadence 5, "a slower controller widens the bridge") pins the optimistic claim. Latent today — the only drive controller attaches at `CadenceTicks: 1` (`driver/driver.go:177`) where the formula is exact — but this number decides "absorbed" vs "void" for the bake gate, so the conservative bridge (`HoldLastTicks`) is the right one to pin.
