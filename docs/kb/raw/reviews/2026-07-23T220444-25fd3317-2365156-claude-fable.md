1. `engine/natsio/sigframe_test.go:288` — flake risk from the new tight upper bound. The window `(sawTick, sawTick+60]` is 60 ticks = 300 ms at the 5 ms pace floor, and it's measured from the watcher's last `NextMsg` return, not from the subscribe instant. On a loaded CI box, `srv.Connect` + `SubscribeSync` slipping past ~300 ms makes a correctly behaving system fail the assert; worse, only ~100 ticks (~500 ms) remain before the run ends, after which the late joiner gets nothing and the 10 s `NextMsg` timeout fires. Cheap hardening: re-sample the watcher's latest snapshot tick *after* the late subscribe and bound against that, or widen the slack. (The old test only had a lower bound, so this failure mode is new.)
