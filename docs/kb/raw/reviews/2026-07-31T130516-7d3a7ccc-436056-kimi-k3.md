  ### Questions

  1. **Clearance elapsed after a lapse is measured from the schedule's hidden onset, not the perceived transition** (`engine/signal.go:284-299` via `sigPhaseAt`). A held amber that lapses into a schedule red whose onset fell mid-hold gets a clearance window measured from the hidden onset — so it can close earlier than `lapse+clearance`, and a hold extending >clearance past the hidden onset yields *no* clearance for what vehicles perceived as amber→red one tick earlier. This fails safe (toward stopping), but the ADR/asyncapi wording "the clearance window covers the transition" is only fully true for supersession (retained histories), not for lapse-into-red. Intended, or worth one sentence of qualification in the ADR note?
