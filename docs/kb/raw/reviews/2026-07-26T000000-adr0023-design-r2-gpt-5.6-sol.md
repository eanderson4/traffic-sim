**Blocker**
- PMTiles is not safely content-addressed: its key uses only network bytes, but output also depends on tippecanoe version/flags, minzoom policy, projection, and derived properties. Different tile bytes could collide at one immutable URL; the exact flags are also deferred rather than pinned. `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:93` `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:399` `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:410`
- Region merging has no completeness barrier. If one subscribed region arrives late, emitting the tick without it and re-emitting later cannot work because `SnapshotBuffer.push` drops duplicate ticks. Initial load and pans can therefore permanently omit visible vehicles. `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:208` `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:304` `viz/src/snapshots.ts:73`
- The recording prefix is not content-addressed to recording contents: stream name plus end sequence detects appends, not replacement/different stores with the same metadata.
