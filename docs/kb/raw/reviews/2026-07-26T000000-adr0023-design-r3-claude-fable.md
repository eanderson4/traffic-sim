Two findings remain, one of which is a real blocker because it re-introduces the round-2 B2 data-loss bug through the round-2 fix for a *different* finding.

**BLOCKER — items 3 and 6 contradict each other on emission during a region stall, and the item-6 reading re-creates B2.**
ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:348-353 says the shim emits EMPTY synthetic frames "below the zoom gate **(and while regions stall, item 3)**". ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:229-232 says stalls *stall emission* (bounded 2 s), then degrade to emitting *without the laggard*. These can't both hold, and the item-6 reading is the dangerous one: `SnapshotBuffer.push` drops any tick ≤ the last pushed (snapshots.ts:73-76), so an empty frame emitted at tick T during a stall permanently forecloses the real frame for T. Concretely, on every pan or initial load above the gate, already-fetched regions cover T but the shim would emit `vehicle_count: 0` — all visible vehicles blink out for up to 2 s and those ticks can never be back-filled. That's exactly the permanent-omission failure the round-2 barrier exists to prevent. Fix is one sentence: empty frames are emitted **only when no region stream is scheduled** (below the gate); above the gate a stall stalls the clock (bounded 2 s), and the degrade path emits the *partial union* of arrived regions, never empty. Delete the parenthetical at line 349.

**SHOULD-FIX — per-chunk emptiness is undefined, and the barrier depends on it.**
"Empty regions are omitted from the index" (ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:207-208) defines known-empty only at whole-region granularity. A region that is non-empty overall but has zero vehicles for a full 60 s window (vehicles passed through early) is unaddressed: does bake write a header-only chunk (120 × 20 B) or omit it from `regions[].frames`? The barrier's "its chunk fetched, or the region known-empty from the index" (line 230-231) has no defined answer for a tick that falls in a chunk-list gap — an implementer could stall forever or mislabel a manifest bug as empty. One sentence fixes it: either every listed region's chunk list is contiguous over `[tickStart, tickEnd]` (empty frames are cheap), or a per-region chunk-lookup miss is defined as known-empty. Same question applies to the TSRL lists (line 300-301).
