  ## Verdicts on the five claims

  **1. "Only speed differs" — TRUE.** Parsed both files: identical type-id sets (nothing added, nothing missing), and per element only the `speed` attribute differs; `numLanes`/`priority`/`oneway`/`width`/`allow`/`disallow`/`discard` are byte-identical to `tools/sumo-venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/sumo/data/typemap/osmNetconvert.typ.xml` (both venv copies are identical to each other). New speeds are a clean mph ladder (13.41=30, 11.18=25, 24.59=55, 20.12=45, 17.88=40, 15.65=35, 6.71=15, 4.47=10). No topology smuggled in.

  **2. A/B explanation — correct mechanism, not post-hoc, but incompletely scoped.** Reproduced from the in-tree JSONs: external lanes 23,833 = 23,833 with identical id sets; internal 31,705 → 31,587 (−118 ✓); successor links −236 ≈ 2×−116 ✓; mean 85.9 → 50.5 km/h, ≥80 km/h 79.7% → 4.3% ✓; the 975 residual lanes are at 88.5 km/h = 55 mph motorway ✓. Exactly 29 external lanes have changed successor sets, and all are renames into renumbered internal-lane ids — drivable topology intact. Losing 116 connections is not a defect. **However**, the deltas are *not* "confined to junction-interior construction" — see should-fix 1.

  **3. Hash shell — sound, no collapse.** `TYPEMAP="${TYPEMAP-...}"` (no colon) deliberately distinguishes unset (→ US map) from set-empty (→ stock); correct use of `-` vs `:-`. `${TYPEMAP:+"$TYPEMAP"}` (import-city.sh:94) is the right idiom — quotes inside the expansion word suppress word splitting even for spaced paths, and unset vs set-empty yield different hash inputs, so the two modes can't collide. Hash covers file *content*, not path. Deterministic on both paths.

  **4. 30 mph statutory — defensible.** IL (625 ILCS 5/11-601), TX (§545.352), GA (§40-6-181), FL (§316.183) all set 30 mph urban/residence-district defaults; CA's 25 mph (CVC 22352) is correctly disclosed. "Accepted rather than forked" is the right call since `maxspeed` still wins. One omission below.

  **5. Pass 2 omission — correct.** Pass 2 reimports a `.net.xml` whose edges carry resolved speeds; `--type-files` only resolves types for typed sources (OSM). Junction recomputation at retyped junctions uses the imported (new) speeds, so interiors stay consistent with pass 1. The comment at scripts/import-city.sh:57-60 is accurate.
