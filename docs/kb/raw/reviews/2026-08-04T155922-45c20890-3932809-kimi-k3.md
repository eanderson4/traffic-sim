  ## Questions

  - **Chain reset on a 1-tick gap** (`engine/sigctl.go:291`): a renewal inherits `chainStart` only when `last.until >= e.Tick`, so a controller that re-commands the *same* phase one tick after each lapse starts a fresh 300 s chain every time — indefinite starvation of the same movement with only single-tick fixed-time gaps, never tripping the cumulative bound. Round-6 scoped "any fixed-time gap = new chain" as an interrupted starvation, and round-8 scoped out deliberate alternating-phase starvation as controller policy — but this is neither alternating nor wedged-loop; it's the minimal variation that defeats the advertised bound. Presumably filed under "actively adversarial controller = policy," but the ADR's rail-scope paragraph doesn't name this case; worth one sentence either way.
  - `signal_lapse` fires at *every* hold end (`sigctl.go:355`), not only at a rail-clamped bound — a routine one-cycle default hold that simply runs its course produces an event named and documented as "the starvation rail firing." If the M2 controller's natural cadence is command-per-cycle without renewal-ahead, that's a perpetual per-cycle record-plane event. Intended volume?
  - Lapse surfacing is confined to `RunLive` (`engine/natsio/run.go:241`); `engine.Run` and the `cmd` harnesses Step the engine without ever reading `SigLapses`, so "never silent" holds only on the NATS path. Acceptable given those paths have no record plane — flagging in case the local-harness story is supposed to match.
