Diff read end-to-end, plus context in `engine/natsio/player.go`, `engine/cmd/replay/main.go`, and the demosrv working tree. No blockers found. Findings:

## Should-fix

**1. `SeekGate` is armed on live streams too — high-pace live runs will reset the buffer every frame.**
`viz/src/main.ts:463` (the snapshot callback) runs `seekGate.observe()` for every run, not just replays. `serve -pace` is unbounded for driverless runs (only >10 refused with clients attached, `engine/cmd/serve/main.go:57`), and at `pace/dt/10 > 240` ticks-per-decimated-frame (e.g. `-pace 300` at dt 0.1 — a plausible "watch while recording" setup) every frame trips the gate: buffer reset + `clearCongestion()` per frame, destroying interpolation and the congestion overlay. Before this diff that scenario lerped fine. Cheap fix: construct/arm the gate only when `cfg.run.endsWith("-replay")` — backward-tick detection is the only shape that means anything on a live stream, and a live stream never goes backward anyway.

**2. `ReplayPanel.poll` hides the panel permanently on a single transient failure.**
`viz/src/replaypanel.ts:268` (`poll()` → `this.hide()` on first null) contradicts the init rationale ("a startup hiccup must not hide the panel for the session", replaypanel.ts:151): one 2 s proxy timeout (`ctlTimeout`, replay.go:29) or one dropped status poll mid-session removes the controls while the replay keeps playing, with no recovery short of a page reload. Require N consecutive failures (or reuse `probeWithRetry`) before hiding.

**3. `serve` absolutizing `spec.Net.Path` bakes machine-local paths into the durable record and the KV meta payload.**
`engine/cmd/serve/main.go:152`: `RunMeta.Spec` is published on the ADR-0006 metadata plane and persisted in the recording; the replay child rebuilds the engine from it (`natsio/player.go:198` → `RestoreState(spec, …)` → `BuildNet(spec.Net)` reads the file). After this change a recording only replays on the machine/checkout that made it — move the repo, rename `$HOME`, or share a store and replay fails with a file-not-found from a path that looks authoritative. The alternative (resolve relative paths at replay time against a `-root`/store-relative base) keeps records portable. If the absolute-path tradeoff is the deliberate call for the local-first demo stage, fine — but it's a payload-semantics change to a published contract value, so per the repo's own rule it deserves at least a commit-message/KB note (the ab8b039 "run meta lifecycle" deferral is adjacent but doesn't cover this).

## Question

**4. Run-id collisions across kinds are allowed — intended?** `registry.go` enforces id uniqueness across demos and recordings but not run uniqueness: a demo with `run: "foo"` and a recording of `run: "foo"` can coexist. Single-active-child makes it safe operationally (serve on `foo` and replay on `foo-replay` never run together), so probably fine — just confirming it's deliberate rather than an oversight in `checkRun`.
