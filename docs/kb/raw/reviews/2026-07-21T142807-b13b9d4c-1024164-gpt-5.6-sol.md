- **blocker** `engine/metrics.go:720` — Metric records hard-code `SchemaVersion: 1` (also `engine/metrics.go:775`), but ADR-0006 requires `schema_version: 2` on every envelope. Publishing these records would create an undeclared incompatible contract.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:118` — `Stops` and `TimeLossS` cannot represent field absence: disabled groups and genuine zero values are identical. ADR-0014 requires selected groups to be present and messages to be self-contained; use optional representation as with `Q/K/V/Occupancy`.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:436` — Despawn presence time is estimated from the previous tick’s speed. Acceleration during the despawn tick makes VHT, occupancy, and time-loss incorrect; the authoritative ballistic motion is not represented by `d/prev.v`.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:323` — A new vehicle crossing a short origin lane before its first observation is attributed wholly to its final lane; one that spawns and despawns in the same tick is omitted entirely (`engine/metrics.go:410`). Valid networks can therefore lose trip records, distance, VHT, and the true origin, violating ADR-0014’s complete-trip/run-total definitions.
