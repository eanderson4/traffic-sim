## Should-fix

1. **`viz/src/glyphs.ts:119-128` — the "SDF" image is binary alpha, not a distance field.** `makeVehicleRectImage` fills a solid rect (alpha 0/255) and registers it `{ sdf: true }`. MapLibre's SDF shader thresholds alpha at ~0.75 with a smoothstep band, so: (a) `icon-halo-width: 1` (main.ts) has no gradient band to occupy — the halo will be an invisible-to-1px filtered fringe, not the intended separation from the navy canvas; (b) under heavy minification (icon-size 0.09 at z11 makes the 16px-wide rect ~1.4px) the thresholded shape erodes and can shimmer or vanish. The comment "the padding gives the SDF field room to fall off" is factually wrong — nothing falls off; padding only prevents edge clamping. Fix: bake a real distance ramp into the alpha channel (even a few inset rects with stepped alpha, or a proper SDF), or drop the halo and the comment's claim.
