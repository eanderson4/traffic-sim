- **blocker** — `engine/natsio/bus.go:306`: Chunking changes the semantics of the existing `state.sig` subject without a versioned migration. A legacy consumer ignores `sig_chunk`, treats every independently valid frame as the whole table, and ultimately retains only the last chunk. This contradicts the “v1 back-compat” and “old clients decode unchanged” claims in `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0016-tssg-chunking.md:48` and `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0016-tssg-chunking.md:101`. Use a versioned subject/schema or explicitly require coordinated consumer migration.
- **should-fix** — `viz/src/nats-client.ts:63`: `RequestStrategy.Timer` keeps every successful multi-reply request open for the full 10 seconds because the responder sends no completion marker. Meanwhile `viz/src/main.ts:345` suppresses further resyncs via `sigReqInFlight`; incomplete replies also cannot retry until timeout. This undermines the ADR’s one-round-trip recovery claim. Use an end-of-replies protocol or an inactivity/jitter strategy.
- **should-fix** — `viz/src/main.ts:345`: The three-attempt budget is permanent unless a request itself completes (`viz/src/main.ts:357`). A later complete broadcast does not reset it, so three transient partial replies disable request-based recovery for the remainder of the page session, including after reconnection or future broadcast gaps.
