- **blocker** `engine/metrics.go:342` — Post-integration lane changes are attributed to the final lane, although `Step` performs integration/boundary movement before `laneChanges`. Pure lateral hops therefore book the whole tick on the new lane; crossing-plus-hop books the successor segment on its neighbor. This violates ADR-0014’s assign-where-traveled boundary split and corrupts per-lane Edie, occupancy, and time-loss metrics.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:410` — Despawn time uses the previous tick’s speed rather than the despawn tick’s ballistic motion. Any acceleration/deceleration makes VHT, occupancy, and time-loss systematically wrong; the constant-speed test at `engine/metrics_test.go:409` does not cover this.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:311` — A vehicle spawned onto a short valid origin lane can cross its boundary before its first observation. This path records only `v.S` on the final lane, losing the origin-lane distance and recording the wrong trip origin. A spawn that also despawns is omitted entirely, as acknowledged at `engine/metrics.go:388`, violating complete-trip/run-total requirements.
- **should-fix** `engine/metrics.go:290` — Pre-placed vehicles are seeded only into `last`, not `tstates`; survivors consequently get `EntryTick = 1` and their post-Step lane as origin, while first-step despawns are reconstructed with `EntryTick = 0` at `engine/metrics.go:428`. Identical initial vehicles thus receive inconsistent trip semantics based solely on whether they survive the first tick.
