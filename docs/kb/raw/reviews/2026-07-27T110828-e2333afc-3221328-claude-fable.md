`bottleneck_town.py:279-285`: `off = -(i+0.5)*LANE_W` along the *left* normal puts higher indices further **right**; verified on the generated network (`main_f0_b0`: idx 0 at y −301.75, idx 2 — the bay — at y −308.75, the kerbside for eastbound). `engine/netfile.go:52` documents `edgeIndex` as "0 = rightmost (SUMO convention)" (merge-pod.py follows it; this generator inverts it). A/B results likely survive — MOBIL is symmetric and the mirroring is identical in every arm — but the network violates the format contract it claims to implement.
