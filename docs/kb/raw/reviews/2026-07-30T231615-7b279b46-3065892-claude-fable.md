**Should-fix**

1. `engine/engine.go:461-465` (`noteLaneLeave`) — cap-then-floor inverts on long lanes: when `freeFlowTime(l) ≥ StrandAfterS` (300 s — e.g. ~2.5 km at 8.3 m/s), every sample is capped to 300 then floored back up to free-flow, so the sample is the constant `ff` and the lane can **never** accumulate congestion evidence — the feature is silently blind exactly on long slow lanes where queues live longest. Rare on urban networks, so defer per the triage bar, but record it: cap should probably be `max(StrandAfterS, ff)` (or cap relative to ff).
