## Should-fix

1. **Cross-round chunk mixing is undetectable under the accepted rules** — `contracts/asyncapi.yaml:130` ("reset the partial set on any gap or regression") and ADR-0016 §4/consequences ("a dropped chunk abandons the round by design"). A contiguous slow-consumer drop spanning a publish-round boundary — lose chunks j+1..n of round k and 1..j of round k+1 — assembles a mixed set with **no gap, no regression, no count change**, and installs. Benign today only because the table is immutable per run (chunks are byte-identical across rounds bar the patched tick); the PROPOSED draft's `sig_gen` existed precisely for this, and the ACCEPTED rewrite dropped it without recording the residual hazard. Cheap contract-only closure: reset accumulation on a mid-group `tick`-header change (all chunks of one live round share a tick, rounds are ≥20 ticks apart, and item 5 removed paused republication). Minimum: record the hazard in ADR-0016's consequences so the mutable-table future work re-introduces generation identity knowingly, instead of inheriting a rule that silently doesn't cover it.
