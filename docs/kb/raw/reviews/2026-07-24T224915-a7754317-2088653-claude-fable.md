The new design *deliberately* deep-links the browser into the map during that build, and the map's replay panel probes `/api/replay/status` on load, so that probe now hangs ~minutes instead of getting a fast `errNoActiveReplay`. A mid-pause attacher whose 3 pulls fail (e.g. a 30 s child stall) renders no signals until resume or reconnect. Consider re-arming the budget on a timer, or counting only consecutive failures within a window.

3. `engine/cmd/demosrv/geojson.go:115` (`netKey`) — every `/net/{id}.geojson` **and every part request** re-reads and sha256s the entire network file. For la-lean that's ~5 serial full reads of a multi-hundred-MB file per client load, added to the client's already-sequential part fetches. The no-memoization discipline is documented, but a short-TTL or mtime-keyed memo of (path → hash) would cut the per-part cost without weakening the mid-edit guarantee materially.
