- **should-fix** `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0013-external-review-gate.md:34` — Accepting rebase/cherry-pick because content was reviewed on its original branch conflicts with binding reviews to the base HEAD. - **should-fix** `hooks/pre-commit:22` — This new commit-critical mechanism has no automated tests for path exemptions, tree/base stamp matching, temporary indexes, or merge hooks.
