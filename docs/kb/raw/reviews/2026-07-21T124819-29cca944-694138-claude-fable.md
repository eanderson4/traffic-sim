Review of the staged diff (docs-only: ADR-0014 draft + two KB index updates). No blockers — this is a PROPOSED ADR, nothing durable binds yet. The KB index edits (`adrs.md`, `gaps-and-roadmap.md`) are internally consistent with each other and with the new file. Findings on the ADR itself:

**Should-fix**

1. **Unfinished trips at horizon are unaccounted — the exact survivorship bias the research warns about.** `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0014-observability-metrics.md:44` emits a trip record only "when a vehicle leaves the network"; `:81-84` builds totals (completed trips, VMT, VHT, total/mean time-loss) and `:117` picks the showcase run by total time-loss. Vehicles still in-network at horizon — precisely the ones suffering the worst congestion — never appear in trip stats, so the most-congested seed can rank as the *least* by total trip time-loss. §3 handles denied-entry demand but not entered-unfinished vehicles, and `congestion-metrics.md:113` flags "averaging only completed trips drops exactly the vehicles suffering the worst congestion." Pin it: emit flagged partial trip records at horizon (SUMO's approach), or define totals over Edie/interval sums rather than trip records — either works, but the ADR must say which, since §3 makes definitions contract text.

2. **v1 emits metrics whose definitions §3 doesn't pin.** §3 (`:56-67`) pins time-loss, Edie cells, interval boundaries, denied-entry — but v1 also ships occupancy, stop count, and stopped time (`:48`, `:54`, `:134-135`) with no normative definition. The stop-speed/duration threshold is a canonical cross-tool discrepancy (the FHWA catalog §3 itself cites), and "occupancy" from trajectories (space-fraction vs time-fraction) is ambiguous — especially since the ADR elsewhere insists time-mean and space-mean are not interchangeable (`:151-153`). Add them to the v1 pin list.

3. **Seconds→ticks conversion and partial final intervals are undefined.** The grammar is `period_s`/`begin_s` in sim seconds (`:92`) but intervals are "aligned to tick 0 and stamped in ticks" (`:62-63`). No rule for `period_s` not being an integral multiple of the tick length, and no statement on whether a final partial interval (horizon not a multiple of period, or `begin_s` offset) is emitted, dropped, or flagged. Both change cross-variant numbers; both are contract semantics under §3.

**Question**

4. **Subject granularity:** `ts.{run}.metrics.interval` carries every measurement set on one subject (`:70-71`). ADR-0006 §3's taxonomy puts entity ids last (`ctl.intent.{controller_id}`), and set ids are stable within the scenario — `metrics.interval.{set_id}` would let the viz scoreboard filter-subscribe instead of parsing every payload. Cheap to decide now; a contract change later.

5. **Authored `metrics/` without `trips: {}`** (`:94`) disables trip records — what do totals and the §7 comparison (median-by-total-time-loss, completed-trip counts) then emit? If trips-off makes sweeps meaningless, say the compare tool refuses or degrades explicitly.

**Nits**

6. `v = q/k` at `:52` is undefined for empty cells (k=0); state what an empty (lane, interval) emits — omitted, null, or zeros.

7. §2 folds occupancy/stops/mean-time-loss *into* the Edie primitive (`:54`) while §5 lists `edie, occupancy, stops, time_loss` as independently selectable names (`:91`) and the default set omits `time_loss` (`:96-97`). Is the lane-interval record one primitive or four toggles? Minor internal inconsistency.

Determinism and contract discipline are otherwise well handled: sorted-order accumulation (§1), ticks-in-payload not subjects (§4, matches ADR-0006 §3), engine-sole-writer, additive channel growth with an explicit info-version bump, and derived-values-excluded-from-CRC are all consistent with ADR-0005/0006. The `ts.{run}.metrics.>` reservation claimed in Context is real (ADR-0006-nats-message-contract.md:31).
