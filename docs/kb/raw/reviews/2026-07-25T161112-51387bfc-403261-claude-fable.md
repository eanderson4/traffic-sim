**Questions:**
- `engine/cmd/demosrv/deploy.go:335-336` vs `supervisor.go:353-359` — if `shutdownFinal` stamps the abort while an autostart's `waitReady` is mid-poll and readiness happens to succeed before the next abort check, autostart logs "demo … is live" and the child is then immediately killed by `stopLocked`. Purely cosmetic log-truth in a dying process; confirm you're fine with it.
- `engine/cmd/demosrv/deploy.go:279-283` — retry envelope is ~14 s total (2+4+8 s). A slow-releasing ws port after a pod reschedule that exceeds that leaves an idle-but-healthy pod (documented keep-serving semantics, and K8s liveness won't catch it since demosrv answers). If that turns out common in practice, the manifests' probe should hit `/api/status` semantics, not just the port — deployment-side note, nothing to change here.
