- **blocker** `engine/natsio/driver/driver.go:420` — Budget deferral makes routing depend on replica sharding and failover timing. `wantLane` is process-local, so a vehicle failing over before admission is redrawn from a later lane; replica count also changes which vehicles receive each replica’s budget. This is newly introduced—not “pre-existing”—and directly violates ADR-0008’s behaviorally invisible failover requirement. It needs a replica-independent admission/input design or an ADR explicitly changing that guarantee.
