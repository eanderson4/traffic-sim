WHAT THE CHANGE IS. netconvert's built-in OSM typemap is German-derived:
unposted highway.secondary / primary / trunk default to 27.78 m/s (100 km/h).
OSM `maxspeed` is tagged on only ~12% of ways generally, and on 19% of
chi-loop's secondary ways specifically, so 12,945 of chi-loop's 15,975
secondary lanes compiled at 62 mph. Measured across the tree, 25 of 28
networks are affected (worst: houston-lean, 51.8% of lanes at exactly
100 km/h). The fix adds an explicit speed-only typemap
(scripts/osm-urban-us.typ.xml) and wires it into scripts/import-city.sh
behind a TYPEMAP variable.
