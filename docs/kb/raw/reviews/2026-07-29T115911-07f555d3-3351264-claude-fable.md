## Blocker

**1. Warm start + demand director can silently double-spawn demand (timing race), breaking the bit-exact guarantee on the flagship path.**
`engine/natsio/demand/director.go:274` (`fastForward`), `engine/natsio/contract.go:299`, `engine/director.go:154`.

`fastForward` skips arrivals with tick ≤ `startTick` only. But the cold run's director sends verbs `Lead` (default 30) ticks early, so at the dump tick the engine's `dirQueue` — which is keyframed and restored — already holds directives with `EarliestTick` in `(startTick, startTick+Lead]`. The warm director re-sends exactly those arrivals with identical request ids (sampler ordinals align, verified against `send()` at director.go:342).

The guards that should absorb the overlap don't fully hold here:
- The contract plane's first-seen dedup (`c.verbs`, contract.go:299) is per-`RunLive` instance and starts **empty** in the warm run. The director's own restart-overlap rationale (director.go:14, "the engine's request-id dedup makes the overlap harmless") assumes the contract plane survives — it does on a director restart, not on a warm start.
- The remaining guard is `EnqueueSpawn`'s uniqueness check against **live** queue entries (engine/director.go:154). It rejects the duplicate only while the original directive is still queued. A re-sent verb delivered after its twin executed (≤ 30 ticks of headroom; arbitrarily little wall time at `-pace 0`, plus hold-and-retry spawns the late duplicate anyway) is **accepted** and spawns a second vehicle — silent demand duplication and CRC divergence, on the path (`serve` + scenario demands, i.e. every Chicago run) the feature exists for.

Both natsio CRC tests (`TestRunLiveWarmStartIsBitExact`, `TestWarmStartRoundTripIsBitExact`) use spawner demand, so the director seam is untested for bit-exactness. Fix direction: seed the contract plane's dedup map (or an engine-side seen-set) from the restored `dirQueue`'s request ids before the run starts — that makes the overlap deterministically harmless regardless of delivery timing — and add a director-attached bit-exact test.
