## Should-fix

1. **`scripts/runreport.py:560` + `:725` — mixed denominators under a subset set, in the exact shape this commit exists to end.** The TOTALS line prints `100 * fw_km / net_km` where `fw_km` has been narrowed to measured corridor lane-km but `net_km` is the full network; the JSON serializes the same mixed pair (`totals.lane_km` = network, `totals.corridor_lane_km` = narrowed). The viz panel independently corrects this (`statspanel.ts` `laneKmBase` uses `coverage.measured_lane_km`), so the text report and the panel print different corridor shares from the same document. Divide by `scope_km`, or serialize which base the share uses.

2. **`scripts/chicago/mkod.py:907` — the "relocated demand cannot be lost" invariant is not actually held.** Injection *sites* are resolved before volume leaves the cut face, but profile resolution is not: with a `--profiles` library lacking a `default`, a portal or interior flow that matches no rule hits `continue` at `mkod.py:991`/`:1044` *after* `realized["freeway"] += rate` (`:971`) and `relocate[corr] += moved` (`:981`). The moved volume is subtracted from the portal and then never emitted, and the printed mass balance / through-share counts flows that do not exist in the file. The `no profile` counters are printed, but nothing ties them back to the veh/h they silently deleted. Either resolve profiles before the relocation split, or make a no-profile flow fatal like an unfired rule is.
