- **blocker** — Cadence validation contradicts itself: the formula gives `dt=0.1` a valid 2 Hz rate, but the next sentence explicitly rejects every `dt < 0.25` while claiming current 0.1-second scenarios work. An implementation cannot follow both rules. `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:186`
- **blocker** — The supposedly content-addressed keys hash inputs rather than output bytes, but omit execution determinants outside ADR-0005’s determinism envelope, notably GOARCH and the exact Brotli/runtime implementation. Different artifact bytes can therefore share an immutable URL. Pin the complete build/platform/compression identity or key from a canonical manifest of output digests. `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:477`, `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0023-baked-replay-pipeline.md:493`, `docs/kb/decisions/ADR-0005-time-model.md:47`
- **should-fix** — TSRL mean generation does not pin accumulation or serialization order.
