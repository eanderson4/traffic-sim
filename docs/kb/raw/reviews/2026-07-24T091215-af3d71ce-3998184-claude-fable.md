When the buffer is starved (`older === newer`) or a vehicle only exists in the newer bracket, `snapshots.ts` reports `speed: 0` and the heading arrives as a raw (unlerped) step. Possible shapes: floor the widening on dtS with a nominal turn-capable speed, or carry the pre-starvation speed.
