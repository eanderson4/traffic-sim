- **blocker** — `engine/cmd/demosrv/main.go:160`: `-wspublic` proceeds after only warning that the advertised broker is unauthenticated and publish-capable. Public clients can publish intents and access JetStream APIs, violating ADR-0006 §9’s observer-read-only contract; the HTTP bearer gate provides no protection.
