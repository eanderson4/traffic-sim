What to look for. (1) Defaults produce a merge bottleneck: lane 1 (bottom mainline lane) jams around the ramp and waves roll upstream — check their slope on the heatmap. (2) Drop a to 0.6 with inflow ~1700: string instability — phantom jams appear without the ramp (set ramp to 0 first). (3) Watch the stuck at lane end counter at high mainline flow: with zero cooperation, mergers run out of acceleration lane. This is the SUMO failure mode we researched, reproduced — and the case for cooperation intents over NATS. (4) Lane changes are instant hops — judge for yourself whether that reads OK visually at 100 ms ticks, or whether we need duration-based lateral motion.
